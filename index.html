Motion 5.2 Alert: 3-Year Contract Lock-In & Unchecked Option Boxes (Pages 65–71)

The proposed Strata Management Agency Agreement in the 2026 AGM packet has three critical issues that owners should amend before passing Motion 5.2: (1) Item 3 locks the scheme into a 3-year term (a 1-year term is standard best practice); (2) Item 5 sets an automatic annual fee escalator of "CPI or 5% (whichever is the greater)" plus up to 7.5% p.a. on Schedule B rates; and (3) Item 6 leaves both Option 1 ($12,500) and Option 2 ($18,000) unticked, while Schedule A1 (page 71) leaves row 2(a) financial management blank—which defaults to "Full authority with no limitations".

Idle Capital Works Cash Earning 0.05% Interest

At 31 August 2026, the scheme held $313,603 in cash, with $270,604 sitting in the Capital Works Fund. Yet the Capital Works Fund earned only $130.06 in interest across the entire 2025/26 financial year (roughly 0.05% p.a.), while 2026/27 Capital Works spending is budgeted at just $42,500. Placing $200,000+ into a term deposit (Schedule D setup fee is $79) could generate $3,900 to $5,900 a year after tax.
